HOW TO FLY

THE LOOP: GATES → LOCK → FIRE → LAND → GRADE

Fly through gold rings — the next ring is labelled with its distance (G-02 523m). The flight-path vector (circle + wings) shows where you are actually going; put it in the ring. Strike: put a warship or radar tower inside the nose diamond and hold it — the lock builds, the diamond goes solid red and the HUD flashes SHOOT. Press SPACE to fire; the missile steers itself in and the target burns. One kill is +1000.

Landings: flaps down, gear down, throttle back, sink under 200 fpm, on the centreline — PAPI shows 2 red + 2 white on the glidepath, PAPI LOCK when you are on it. Watch the numbers: fuel burns with throttle (twice as fast in afterburner), over 6.0 G damages the airframe, and the storm front (ETA timer) closes the coast. In the storm, rain slants with the wind and lightning is real — don't fly through the front.
